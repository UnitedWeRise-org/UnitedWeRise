UnitedWeRise.org Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 31, 2026 | Last Updated: March 31, 2026

This policy governs the collection, use, retention, and disclosure of information gathered through UnitedWeRise.org, a civic engagement platform operated by People United for Peaceful Revolution, Inc. ("PUPR"), a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization (collectively, "UWR," "we," or "us"). For privacy practices specific to the petition portal, see our Petition Privacy Policy.

Our Commitment

UWR was conceived to protect and expand rights of speech, political association, and civic participation. This commitment extends to how we handle the data of every person who uses this platform. We do not sell your data. We do not use your data for advertising. We collect only what is necessary to operate the platform and to provide the civic engagement features you use.

Information We Collect

Account information you provide: Email address (required for account creation), username and password, and optionally your first name, last name, and address (used for representative lookup and civic features).

Content and activity: Posts, comments, and messages you create; likes, follows, and other interactions; representative searches and civic engagement activities; petition signatures and related submissions.

Technical information collected automatically: IP address, device information, browser type and settings, usage analytics, and error logs.

Information from third-party services: If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture from Google. If you use civic features, your address may be checked against public data sources to identify your elected representatives.

How We Use Your Information

Core platform functions: Providing account access and authentication, displaying your content to other users, finding your elected representatives, and personalizing your civic engagement experience.

Communications: Sending account verification and password reset emails, notifying you of platform activity, and providing support.

Platform improvement: Analytics to improve user experience, security monitoring and fraud prevention, and troubleshooting technical issues.

Information Sharing

Public information. The following is publicly visible: your username, display name, and profile information; posts and comments you create; follower and following relationships; and political profile type (if you are a candidate or official).

Third-party services. We share limited information with service providers that help us operate the platform:

  • Google Maps and Civic Information API: Addresses for representative lookup.
  • Geocodio API: Addresses for enhanced civic data.
  • hCaptcha: Security verification data.
  • Microsoft Azure: Cloud hosting infrastructure for all platform data.
  • Stripe: Payment processing for donations (Stripe receives payment details directly; we do not store your card information).

These providers receive only the data necessary to perform their specific function and are governed by their own privacy policies and data processing agreements. We do not authorize these providers to use your data for any purpose other than the service they provide to UWR.

Legal requirements. We may disclose information when required by law, court order, or to protect the safety of users or the public. See "Responses to Legal Process" below.

Responses to Legal Process

We conceived this platform to protect rights of speech and association. We will do our best to resist government pressure, but UWR will comply with valid legal process, including subpoenas and court orders. In such cases:

  • UWR will comply only with the minimum scope required by the legal process.
  • UWR will notify affected users before producing records, where legally permitted and feasible.
  • UWR will challenge overbroad or legally deficient requests where resources permit.

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including:

  • Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Secure password hashing (bcrypt)
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Access controls and authentication

Data Retention

Account data: We retain your information as long as your account is active. If you request account deletion, your personal data will be removed within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

Technical logs: Server logs and analytics data are retained for 90 days, then permanently deleted.

Content: Posts and comments you create remain on the platform until you delete them or your account is closed.

Legal holds. In the event of pending or anticipated litigation, regulatory inquiry, or valid legal process, UWR may extend retention of relevant data beyond the standard retention period. Any such extension will be documented and lifted when the legal matter concludes.

Your Rights and Choices

Account control: You may update your profile information at any time, delete posts and comments you have created, and control who can follow you or message you.

Privacy settings: You may make your profile private or public, control notification preferences, and opt out of analytics tracking.

Data requests: You may request a copy of your personal data, correction of inaccurate information, or deletion of your account and data by contacting us at contact@unitedwerise.org.

Children's Privacy

United We Rise is not intended for users under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

International Users

United We Rise is based in the United States. By using the platform, you consent to the transfer and processing of your information in the United States.

Content Disclaimer

The views, statements, and opinions expressed in user-generated content on the UWR platform are those of the individual users. UWR does not endorse, verify, or take responsibility for user-generated content, and hosting content on this platform does not constitute UWR's endorsement of its claims or positions.

Changes to This Policy

UWR may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the platform after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
