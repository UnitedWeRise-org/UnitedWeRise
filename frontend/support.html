Contact & Support

United We Rise is operated by People United for Peaceful Revolution, Inc. ("PUPR"), a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization. We are here to help with any questions or concerns about the platform.

For all inquiries, reach us at:

contact@unitedwerise.org

We aim to respond within 2 business days.

How We Can Help

Our team handles inquiries across all areas of the platform. When contacting us, it helps to mention which area your question relates to:

  • Account support — Account recovery, login issues, profile changes, or account deletion requests.
  • Privacy and data requests — Requesting a copy of your data, correcting information, or exercising your rights under our Privacy Policy.
  • Content and moderation — Reporting content, appealing moderation decisions, or questions about community guidelines.
  • Candidate and campaign support — Candidate verification, campaign features, or questions about the candidate system.
  • Petition support — Questions about creating or signing petitions, petition data, or the Petition Privacy Policy.
  • Donations — Donation receipts, refund requests, or questions about financial contributions.
  • Security vulnerabilities — If you have discovered a security vulnerability, please include "SECURITY" in the subject line so we can prioritize your report.
  • Legal inquiries — Legal process, DMCA requests, or formal legal matters.
  • General feedback — Feature suggestions, bug reports, or general questions about the platform.
